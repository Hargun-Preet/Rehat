- Training period: 2025-09-01 00:00:00 to 2025-11-13 23:00:00 (26,287 station-hours).
- Held-out test period (last 3 months, never used for fitting): 2026-11-03 00:00:00 to 2026-11-09 22:00:00.
- Test hours scored: 1,790 station-hours inside the insured window (Mon to Sat, 08:00 to 18:00).

## What this means

Band agreement near the payout threshold is poor. The rebuild is good enough to estimate how often a station crosses the thresholds over many years (the back-test), but NOT good enough to settle a single hour. Payouts always use NEA's observed readings; the rebuild is used only for normals, pricing and forecasting.
